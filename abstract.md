# Project Abstract: FoodCovering 2.0

**Title:** FoodCovering — A Smart Food Safety, Tracking, and Donation Platform

**Abstract:**

Global food waste and food insecurity remain two of the most pressing socio-economic and environmental challenges of the modern era. While substantial amounts of food are discarded daily by restaurants, supermarkets, and individuals, millions of people simultaneously lack access to adequate nutrition. **FoodCovering 2.0** is an innovative, full-stack web application engineered to bridge this gap by establishing a streamlined, real-time ecosystem for safe food donation and redistribution.

At its core, FoodCovering leverages simulated Artificial Intelligence (AI) and live-data pipelines to ensure that donated food meets rigorous safety standards before it reaches vulnerable populations. The platform features an **AI Food Quality Analysis** module that evaluates uploaded images of food items to determine freshness, spoilage risk, and estimated shelf life using visual indicators. This proactive safety layer prevents hazardous or spoiled food from entering the donation supply chain.

To facilitate efficient logistics, FoodCovering integrates a **Live Real-Time Tracking and Map View** powered by websockets and interactive geographic mapping (React-Leaflet). This system tracks active donors, on-duty volunteers, and storage facilities dynamically across the city grid. Volunteers are routed optimally, and end-to-end transparency is maintained through live ETA updates and step-by-step timeline tracking.

The administrative interface provides a robust, responsive **Dashboard** offering comprehensive data visualization—tracking donation volumes, food category distributions, system health, and processing statistics in real-time. Designed with a modern, high-fidelity user experience utilizing React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Zustand for state management, FoodCovering represents a highly scalable, tech-driven solution to minimize urban food waste while maximizing safe, community-driven food recovery.
