# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Name:** FoodCovering
**Mission:** A modern food-safety and smart-city platform to analyze food freshness, manage donations, and track food delivery using AI.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Donors:** Supermarkets, restaurants, individuals wanting to donate surplus food.
- **Receivers:** Shelters, food banks, individuals in need.
- **Volunteers:** People helping transport the food.
- **Administrators/Officers:** Government or organizational officers monitoring food safety and managing complaints.

## 3. Core Features
1. **AI Food Analysis:** Analyze food images for freshness, spoilage risk, and estimated shelf life.
2. **Donation Management:** Create, track, and manage food donations.
3. **Live Tracking:** Real-time map tracking of volunteers and food transport.
4. **Complaint Management:** Log and investigate food safety complaints.
5. **Multilingual Support:** English, Tamil, and Hindi translations.
6. **Live Data Pipeline:** Real-time metrics and updates via WebSocket.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Fast load times, responsive UI.
- **Security:** Secure authentication and role-based access.
- **Usability:** Professional, clean, accessible design.
- **Scalability:** Architecture capable of scaling with increasing users and data.
